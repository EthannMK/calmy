## Headline

The product is further along than "70%". Rules, risk scoring, payouts, points, the review queue, chat with human takeover and roles all work and are well tested. The real problems were in the **hand-offs between the two apps**: the traveler could not see an insurer's decision without reloading, a declined claim's "Ask for a second review" button always failed, the insurer had no way to ask the traveler a question, and there was no shared record of what happened on a claim. Those are fixed. One endpoint could pay a claim twice; that is fixed too.

## Deliverable B: workflows and claim lifecycle

### Claim states (as built; no new status values were added)

```
Policy:   quote --checkout--> active --on time / landed--> archived

Delay claim (decisions.status):
  flight event, delay >= rule, risk < threshold --> paid         (system)
  flight event, delay >= rule, risk >= threshold -> held         (system, never declined by the system)
  delay under rule, traveler asks re-check -----> held          (traveler, note attached)
  held --reviewer asks a question--> held + "waiting on traveler" (reviewer; event info_requested)
  held --approve--> paid (reviewer name signed)   held --decline--> declined (reviewer)
  declined --traveler asks second review (once)--> held   then final after a second decline
  paid --delay grows past tier 2--> paid, amount topped up (system, once)

Lost bag: pending/held --approve--> paid | --decline--> declined   (always a person)
```
Every transition is in `app/engine.py` and writes one row to the `events` table, which is both the audit log and the case history.

### Cross-role data flow

```
TRAVELER APP                         BACKEND (one claim id)                    INSURER CONSOLE
Demo controls / webhook ──────────▶ engine.run_delay → decisions row ───────▶ Review queue (polls 2.5s)
"Ask us to check again" ──────────▶ engine.request_recheck → held ──────────▶ "Traveler asked" chip
                                    (declined → request_second_review)        "Second review" chip
Messages ◀───────────────────────── chat (staff msg tagged with claim id) ◀── "Send to the traveler"
Claim screen (polls 4s) ◀────────── decide_held → paid/declined + message ◀── Approve / Decline
"What happened" card ◀───────────── engine.case_history(events) ──────────▶ "Case history" panel
```

## Deliverable C and E: what changed

| Fix | What was wrong | What changed | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second review | Button always failed; old endpoint unlimited | `engine.request_second_review` reopens the same claim once, with the traveler's note and the earlier decision on file. `request_recheck` routes declined claims to it, so the existing button works. Home only offers it while allowed; afterwards it shows **Message us**. The decline message no longer promises a review that is not available. | `app/engine.py`, `app/mobile.py`, `app/main.py`, `static/traveler.html` |
| Live phone | Phone never refreshed | Every 4 s the phone re-reads its claims on Home, Claims, a trip or a claim, never while typing, and only re-draws when something changed. A banner says when a claim was approved or decided. | `static/traveler.html` |
| Replay double pay | Claim deleted, points kept | Replay is refused when the trip already has a claim. Nothing is deleted. | `app/main.py` |
| Ask the traveler | Draft could only be copied | `POST /api/console/review/{id}/ask` (reviewer role) puts the question in the traveler's Messages under the reviewer's name, hands that chat to the reviewer so the answer reaches a person, updates the claim card, and logs it. Queue shows **Waiting on traveler** until the claim is reopened or decided. | `app/main.py`, `static/console.html` |
| Shared history | No per-claim record | `engine.case_history` reads the audit log for one trip. Traveler sees plain steps (`GET /api/app/history/{policy}`, own trips only); the review screen shows the same steps plus risk score and reviewer notes. Decisions are also posted to the traveler's Messages, tagged with the claim id. | `app/engine.py`, `app/mobile.py`, `app/main.py`, both pages |
| Bag messages | No traveler message | Approve/decline now writes one and posts it to Messages. | `app/engine.py`, `app/main.py` |
| Demo lockdown | Presenter tools always open | Off when demo mode is off; optional webhook secret. Demo behaviour unchanged. | `app/auth.py`, `static/traveler.html` |

On the guide you shared (could not be read directly; LinkedIn blocks automated access): Calmy already covers the usual insurance-app checklist. Claim tracking with a visible history was the one item both relevant and missing, so it is the only feature added. Document upload was left out on purpose, because "zero paperwork" is part of the pitch.
