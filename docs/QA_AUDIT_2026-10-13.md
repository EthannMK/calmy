## D. Remaining risks

- Demo routes (`/api/demo/*`) and the flight webhook stay open in demo mode by design. Anyone can reset the shared demo or trigger a payout on it. Two judges at once share one database.
- `POST /api/flights/{code}/refresh` and `/api/outlet/scan` need no sign-in: the first can use up the live flight budget, the second can mark a voucher used by its code.
- State is lost on every deploy or restart, and the app must run as one instance.
- A payout on an *estimated* departure can be made before the flight leaves, and is never reduced if the estimate shrinks (D3).
- Claim timelines carry fixed illustrative step times (0s, 1s, 3s, 8s). The measured time is on the Overview.

## E. Decisions for the product owner

**Before the demo**

- **D1. Cancellation after a delay payout.** A flight paid S$40 at 3 hours and then cancelled stays at S$40, though a cancellation pays S$80. Options: top up to the cancellation amount (fairer, matches "cancelled pays S$80"); or keep the first payout (simpler, current). Recommendation: top up. Not blocking.
- **D2. Delay reported after "on time".** Today it is recorded but not paid. Options: send it to the review queue (safe, a person decides); pay automatically (fast, but an out-of-order data feed could pay wrongly); ignore (current, now visible). Recommendation: review queue. Not blocking.

**Can wait**

- **D3. Which time decides a delay.** Departure, using actual time or the airline's estimate. Options: actual departure only (certain, slower); estimate allowed (instant, may pay a delay that shrinks); arrival delay (closer to the traveler's loss). Current: departure, estimate allowed, no clawback.
- **D4. Re-check amount.** A re-check approved by a person always pays the tier-1 amount, even if the airline record shows 6 hours. Options: let the reviewer pick the tier; recompute from the delay the reviewer enters. Current: tier 1 only.
- **D5. Second reviews.** One second review per declined claim is allowed, then the decision is final. Is one right?

**Production and compliance**

- **D6.** Real payment rails, with separate "approved" and "paid" states and failed-payment handling (today payment is instant and simulated).
- **D7.** Persistent database and multiple instances; closing the demo routes and signing the webhook (`KODA_WEBHOOK_SECRET` already supported).
- **D8.** Whether travelers may see their own risk signals (today they do not).

## F. Recommendation

Ready for the hackathon demo once these fixes are deployed: the traveler and insurer journeys, money paths and AI guardrails behave correctly in every scenario run. Next: deploy, then decide D1 and D2.
