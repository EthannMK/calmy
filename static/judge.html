A flight is late. The traveler is paid in seconds.

KODA is cover that pays by itself when a flight is delayed or cancelled. Flightwise Insurance sells it, KODA AI runs it. This page shows both sides of one trip at the same time.

Left: the travelerThe phone app. Pick a real flight, add cover, get paid.
Right: the insurerFlightwise Insurance's desk. Every decision and the reason for it.
Top bar: your four stepsEach button does the step for you. Real flight data, simulated payout.
  1. Cover a tripPick a real upcoming flight and add cover. A second traveler with a suspicious profile joins the same flight.
  2. Break the flightAct as the airline's status feed: build a storm, delay or cancel. The first traveler gets an early alert, a notification and a payout in seconds, then can claim extra costs with a receipt and draft a letter to the airline.
  3. See the held claimThe same delay hits the suspicious traveler. It is not paid. It goes to a person with the reason.
  4. Decide as the insurerOpen the review queue, read the copilot's advice, then approve or decline. Extra cost receipts are in Extra costs.
