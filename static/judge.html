KODA

Your flight is delayed.
Your payout isn't.

A delay is already a fact in an airline data feed, so the claim should not exist. KODA is flight cover that pays by itself, with an insurer desk where a person sees every decision and the reason behind it.

The problem today

A delay claim takes about 14 days and costs an insurer roughly US$41 to settle by hand.

So a small payout is not worth the forms. Most travelers never claim, the insurer keeps premium it never pays out, and the customer decides insurance does not work.

At the desk the claim arrives as email and attachments, with no single view of the case and no record of why it was flagged.

What the AI does

A trained model on 336,776 real flights scores the delay risk of every flight, so the traveler is warned early and the insurer can price and reserve for it.

Published rules pay automatically. A readable risk score decides when to stop and ask a person.

Three agents do what rules cannot: investigate a held case with read-only tools, advise the reviewer with the evidence, and answer the traveler from their own policy, handing over to a person on request.

No model decides money. The system can pay or hold. Only a person can decline.
